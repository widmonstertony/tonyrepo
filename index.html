ThermalLightControl 3.7.1

One universal arm64/arm64e package prevents thermal brightness caps on iPadOS 16–17 while preserving CPU/GPU throttling, temperature monitoring, warnings, watchdog, and emergency shutdown.

Safety: sustained high brightness at elevated temperatures increases power consumption, display wear, and overheating risk. Monitor device temperature and disable the tweak if the device becomes unusually hot.
