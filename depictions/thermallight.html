ThermalLightControl 3.7.1

Prevents thermal brightness caps on iPadOS 16–17 rootless jailbreaks while preserving CPU/GPU throttling, temperature monitoring, thermal warnings, watchdog, and emergency shutdown.

What's new

Version 3.7.1 combines arm64 and arm64e in one package. It covers the verified CoreBrightness, CoreAnimation, EDR, notification, and per-frame RTPLC routes. iPadOS 16.1 arm64e devices retain the build-gated final-cap hook; iPadOS 17 uses the validated generic routes without a fixed private-framework offset.

Compatibility

Verified on iPadOS 16.1 arm64e and iPadOS 17.7.11 A10X arm64 with Dopamine rootless and ElleKit. The package requires iOS/iPadOS 16.0 through 17.x.

Controls

The Settings app contains a bilingual on/off switch. The command-line utility supports on, off, status, and check.

Warning: sustained high brightness at elevated temperatures can increase power consumption, display wear, and overheating risk. Monitor device temperature and disable the tweak if the device becomes unusually hot.
